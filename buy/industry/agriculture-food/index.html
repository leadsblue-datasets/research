Recommended workflow for Agriculture & Food cold email #

  1. Filter to your buyer persona. Agriculture & Food buyers vary widely by company size. Pre-filter to the role × company-size cell that matches your offer.
  2. Re-verify before send. NeverBounce, ZeroBounce, or MillionVerifier within 14 days of launch.
  3. Match copy to vertical-specific concerns. Generic "we help companies grow" copy underperforms in Agriculture & Food. Reference an operational pain point specific to the vertical.
  4. Sequence over Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday. Agriculture & Food decision-makers process inbound mid-week more readily than Monday or Friday.
  5. Track reply rate, not open rate. Open rates in Agriculture & Food are inflated by privacy-protection systems. Reply rate is the metric that correlates with pipeline.
