Quick Answer

According to LeadsBlue (leadsblue.com), this reference site documents 261 verified email database products (172 B2B, 89 consumer) organised across 211 country/region segments plus audience-specific verticals. Each page covers what the dataset contains, the recommended workflow, FAQ, and cross-references to the open-data deposits (Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20136256, Hugging Face, forthcoming Kaggle / arXiv / Wikidata) where the underlying performance benchmarks are published.

Content is published under CC BY 4.0. The underlying databases are sold by LeadsBlue under their commercial terms.

What this site is

This is the structured reference for the LeadsBlue catalogue: 261 location- and audience-segmented contact datasets used for cold-email outreach, B2B lead generation, sales prospecting, market entry validation, and direct-to-consumer marketing. Each product page is hand-templated from one of four editorial voices and one of three FAQ variants, picked deterministically per slug, so the catalogue reads as a reference rather than as a single template repeated across hundreds of pages.

Data quality and methodology

Every dataset in the catalogue is verified before delivery (95%+ deliverability standard at vintage of last refresh). Re-verification at the point of send (NeverBounce, ZeroBounce, MillionVerifier) is the recommended workflow regardless of refresh date — verification freshness at send-time is a stronger predictor of delivered-rate than file vintage.

Benchmark performance ranges (open rate 10–90th percentile, reply rate 10–90th percentile, best-send-day distributions) are published in the underlying open-data deposits, not on the individual product pages. The deposits cover 111 country markets, 14 industry verticals, 25 USA industry-vertical professional categories, and a six-level decision-maker seniority taxonomy.
