permissive tier — what India email-marketing law requires #

India sits in the permissive compliance tier. In practice, this permits B2B cold contact with sender identification and a working opt-out.

Regulator: MeitY (DPDP Act 2023).

Standard requirements across most jurisdictions, India included:

  1. Sender identification. Your "From" name and reply-to must accurately identify your company. No misleading subject lines.
  2. Working unsubscribe. Every email needs a one-click or one-step opt-out. Honour requests within the legal window (most regimes: 10 business days).
  3. Honest content. Subject lines must reflect the body; no bait-and-switch.
  4. Recipient address legitimacy. Send to verified business addresses only.

This page is not legal advice. Read MeitY (DPDP Act 2023)'s published guidance before launching a sequence, or consult a privacy lawyer in ambiguous cases.

Recommended workflow for India cold email #

  1. Re-verify the list at the point of send. Run the CSV through NeverBounce, ZeroBounce, or MillionVerifier within 14 days of launch. Drop hard bounces. This is the single highest-impact deliverability step.
  2. Authenticate the sending domain. SPF + DKIM + DMARC all passing. India ESPs (and global gatekeepers like Gmail and Outlook) increasingly reject unauthenticated cold sends.
  3. Build a 3–5 touch sequence. Space touches 4–7 days apart. Indian B2B buyers are highly email-responsive relative to other Asian markets — cold email open rates are among the highest globally (20-28%) because digital outreach is an established commercial norm.
  4. Send during Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, 10:00am-12:00pm local time. Timezone alignment matters more than absolute send time — schedule against the recipient's local morning.
  5. Track open, reply, bounce, unsubscribe. Iterate on subject lines first, opening lines second, sequence cadence third.

Decision-maker context. Indian B2B purchasing involves multiple layers of approval. The owner or MD has final authority in SMEs; enterprise companies involve a Purchase Committee and often require vendor empanelment (official approval listing). Titles to target: MD, CEO, GM-IT, VP Sales, Purchase Manager, CTO. Price negotiation is expected and aggressive — initial offers should leave room. Relationship establishment before formal proposals is critical; cold email works best as an introduction to request a call, not to close.
