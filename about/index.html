What this site is #

This site is a buyer catalogue — 100 pages indexing the verified B2B email databases that LeadsBlue.com sells, with each page documenting the real performance benchmark for that segment.

The data behind every benchmark number on this site is published openly under CC BY 4.0 — on Zenodo (canonical) and on Hugging Face. That means anyone can verify, audit, contest, or build on the numbers. Nothing on these pages depends on you taking our word for it.

Relationship to LeadsBlue.com #

This site is operated by LeadsBlue Analytics LTD, the same legal entity that operates leadsblue.com. We don't pretend the relationship is arms-length. What we've done instead is make the data underlying every claim independently citable, so any number on this site can be checked against the published dataset by anyone with five minutes and a CSV reader.

The page content (CC BY 4.0) and the contact databases (commercial product, sold by LeadsBlue under its own terms) are separate things:

  • Page content & dataset — free to reuse, redistribute, adapt. Just cite the DOI.
  • Contact databases — sold by LeadsBlue under commercial terms. Pricing per segment.

Methodology #

Brief summary; full methodology is in the Zenodo deposit:

  1. Aggregation. B2B cold-email campaign performance is aggregated across sending platforms (Instantly, Smartlead, Apollo, native SMTP). Identifying details are stripped at the campaign level; only segment-level aggregates are retained.
  2. Normalisation. Open and reply rates are normalised against verified-list baselines — unverified-list outliers are reported separately, not folded into the headline numbers.
  3. Segmentation. Three primary axes: country (74 segments), industry vertical (14 segments), job seniority (7 tiers). Cross-segment cells are reported only when the underlying sample size meets the published threshold.
  4. Compliance tier classification. Each country is classified as permissive / moderate / strict against four jurisdictional dimensions (consent threshold, B2B carve-outs, opt-out window, regulator enforcement record). The classification rubric is in the methodology document.
  5. Refresh cadence. Country and industry segments are reviewed quarterly. Compliance classifications are reviewed when the underlying law changes (latest review: 2026-05-12).

Known limitations: skews toward English-language campaigns; sampling under-represents Africa and parts of Latin America; data prior to 2024 is excluded due to deliverability regime changes (Gmail/Yahoo sender requirements, Apple Mail Privacy Protection).

FAQ #

Who is LeadsBlue Research?
LeadsBlue Research is the editorial and dataset-publication arm of LeadsBlue Analytics LTD. It publishes the open B2B Cold Email Benchmark Dataset and this catalogue, with the explicit goal of making segment-specific cold-email performance data citable and reproducible.
Is this site independent from LeadsBlue.com?
No — and it doesn't claim to be. This catalogue is published by LeadsBlue Analytics LTD (the same legal entity that operates leadsblue.com) and is the buyer-facing index of LeadsBlue's commercial database products. The benchmark dataset behind the numbers is independently citable (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20136256, CC BY 4.0) and can be verified or contested by anyone with the underlying data. We've published it openly precisely so the numbers don't have to be taken on trust.
How was the benchmark data produced?
Aggregated B2B cold-email campaign performance data, normalised across send platforms (Instantly, Smartlead, Apollo, native SMTP), de-identified at the campaign level, and segmented by recipient country, industry vertical, and seniority tier. Methodology and limitations are documented in the Zenodo deposit.
Can I reuse the data and the page content?
Yes. Both the benchmark dataset and the page content on this site are licensed under CC BY 4.0. You can redistribute, adapt, and build on the material — including commercially — as long as you cite the source. The contact databases sold by LeadsBlue are a separate commercial product and not covered by the CC license.
How is this site different from b2bdataindex.com?
b2bdataindex.com is the open Q&A surface — 250 pages answering specific benchmark questions ("what's the cold email open rate in Germany?"). This site is the buyer-intent surface — segment pages for people deciding which B2B email list to buy. Both reference the same underlying dataset and cross-link to each other.
Who is Luther Johnson?
Principal researcher for LeadsBlue Research. ORCID 0009-0008-9836-1280. Author of record on the Zenodo deposit (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20136256) and editor of this catalogue.
How do I cite a page from this site?
For a specific page, cite the page URL and date accessed. For the benchmark data underlying the numbers, cite the dataset: Johnson, L. (2026). B2B Cold Email Benchmark Report 2026. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20136256
How do I report an error or contest a number?
Email research@leadsblue.com with the page URL, the specific claim, and (if you have it) the data that supports the correction. Errata are documented on the Zenodo deposit and pages are updated within the published refresh cadence.
