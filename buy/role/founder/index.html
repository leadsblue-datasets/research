Writing to Founders — what works #

  1. Subject line: 3–5 words, metric-anchored. "Q4 churn at " works; "Quick question" doesn't.
  2. First line: name the operational concern, not the product. Founder screens for relevance before reading anything else.
  3. Second line: one piece of evidence. A number, a comparable customer, a published claim. Verifiable.
  4. Close: yes-or-no question. "Worth a 15-minute call this week?" — not "would love to find time to discuss…"
  5. Total length: under 100 words. Founders read on mobile in inbox previews. Long emails get archived unread.

Founder email list — FAQ #

What's the typical reply rate for cold email to Founders?
Well-targeted campaigns to Founders achieve 0.8–2.5% reply rates (median 1.7%) per the B2B Cold Email Benchmark Dataset (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20136256). Founder replies cluster at the upper band when the email's "ask" is calibrated to company founder / co-founder authority — a yes-or-no question with a one-sentence ROI claim outperforms a discovery-call ask by roughly 2x in this segment.
What should I write to a Founder in a cold email?
One concrete operational claim relevant to Founder authority, one piece of supporting evidence, one yes-or-no question. Subject lines that work for Founders are short (3–5 words), reference a specific metric or quarter, and don't try to manufacture urgency. Body should fit on a single screen.
